2) Accomplished During This Meeting
- Each member presented their summaries. Key takeaways recorded:
  - BKT is a two-state HMM per skill with learn, guess, slip (and optionally forget) parameters; it fits with very few learners, which matters because we have a handful of models, not thousands of students.
  - Deep KT (DKT, AKT) needs thousands of learner sequences and is a black box; it can only be an ablation for us.
  - Fluid Benchmarking fits static 2PL IRT per checkpoint and releases item parameters; MathCAMPS gives 44 Common Core skills but only reports accuracy curves.
- First discussion of the central design question: for an LLM, the benchmark items are observations, not practice. The model does not learn from the test. So the "time step" should be the checkpoint, with all items at a checkpoint treated as simultaneous noisy readings. Agreed to bring this to the advisor as Decision 1.
- Agreed a tentative two-phase plan: Phase 1 on public checkpoint data with MMLU subjects as skills; Phase 2 on OLMo-2 with MathCAMPS.
