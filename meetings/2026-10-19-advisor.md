3) Issues / Blockers
- The per-skill temporal model cannot beat the item-mean bar at ranking within a skill; the cause is structural (one ability per skill plus a fixed per-question offset vs. one free number per question). A model with drifting per-question residuals is proposed.
- GPU access for the Phase 2 run (OLMo-2 on MathCAMPS) still unresolved; fallback datasets identified.
- BKT needs a custom log-space implementation with per-item emissions (pyBKT underflows at our batch sizes; one guess/slip pair per skill gives within-skill AUC 0.50 by construction).
